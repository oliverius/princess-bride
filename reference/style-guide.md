# The Princess Bride — Interactive Fiction Visual Style Guide

**Project:** Unofficial, non-commercial fan-made Twine interactive fiction  
**Visual direction:** Vibrant, cinematic, 1980s-inspired anime/manga  
**Purpose:** Maintain consistent character designs, environments, and illustrations across story passages.

> This document describes the established *illustrated adaptations* for this project, not exact likenesses of the film actors. Use the approved visual reference sheet alongside these written specifications. Where a generated reference sheet contains an inconsistent face, prefer the character specification below.

## 1. Art direction

- **Look:** Hand-drawn anime with confident, clean outlines; expressive eyes; simplified but strong cel-shaded shadows; subtle painted detail in backgrounds.
- **Mood:** Swashbuckling adventure, earnest romance, theatrical villainy and playful humour. Avoid grimdark realism.
- **Colour:** Saturated pink/coral/violet sunset skies, cobalt daylight, warm gold highlights, verdant hills, blue-violet shadows.
- **Lighting:** Strong directional lighting, readable silhouettes, warm rim light and cinematic contrast.
- **Rendering:** Consistent proportions and facial features; avoid photorealistic skin, 3D rendering, excessive texture and washed-out palettes.
- **Default frame:** Landscape **16:9** for story passages. Use close-ups and portrait crops for character cards when needed.
- **No embedded text:** No dialogue, speech bubbles, captions, labels, watermarks or UI. Twine supplies accessible text and choices separately.

## 3. Character model specifications

### Westley / the Dread Pirate Roberts

- **Silhouette:** Lean, athletic, agile; tall but not imposing; understated heroism.
- **Face:** Youthful and angular, narrow jaw, straight nose, bright blue eyes; warm, restrained smile.
- **Hair:** Tousled **golden blond**, layered and windblown. This is a defining trait in our anime adaptation.
- **Clothing:** Dark charcoal/black lace-up tunic, fitted trousers, boots, sword belt and a dark flowing cloak. For masked-pirate scenes, add black eye mask and head covering while retaining his slender build.
- **Movement:** Balanced, light-footed, precise; confident without swagger.
- **Expression set:** Tender smile, dry amusement, focused determination, quiet concern.
- **Distinct from Humperdinck:** Westley wears **dark, practical clothing**, has a softer youthful face, tousled hair, and an agile swordsman's silhouette; **never dress him in red royal regalia**.

### Buttercup

- **Silhouette:** Graceful, natural posture; confident rather than fragile.
- **Face:** Soft oval face, large clear blue eyes, warm expressive features.
- **Hair:** Long flowing chestnut/auburn hair, loose waves, sometimes a delicate floral headband or braid.
- **Clothing:** Cream/ivory medieval gown with restrained gold embroidery; simpler rural dress for farm scenes; more formal attire for palace scenes.
- **Movement:** Calm, dignified; emotions conveyed through gaze and posture.
- **Expression set:** Fondness, scepticism, grief, resolve, astonishment.
- **Avoid:** Overly ornate princess accessories in farm scenes, or an infantilised appearance.

### Inigo Montoya

- **Silhouette:** Slim, wiry, exceptionally poised swordsman.
- **Face:** Distinctly angular, prominent brows, intense brown eyes, **narrow dark moustache**, mature features.
- **Hair:** Long, wavy **dark brown** hair to the shoulders, loose and wind-swept.
- **Clothing:** Off-white rolled-sleeve shirt, brown leather vest, fitted dark trousers, boots and an ornate rapier.
- **Movement:** Elegant fencing stances, controlled gestures, theatrical courtesy.
- **Expression set:** Determined stare, wry half-smile, reflective sadness, fierce anger, exhausted relief.
- **Consistency rule:** **Every expression thumbnail must show Inigo himself**: same narrow face, dark wavy hair and moustache. Do **not** substitute Fezzik's broad face or bulky physique.

### Fezzik

- **Silhouette:** Extremely tall and broad, towering above companions; gentle giant.
- **Face:** Broad friendly face, thick brows, kind eyes, rounded features.
- **Hair:** Thick, curly dark brown hair.
- **Clothing:** Loose muted mauve/grey tunic, broad belt, rugged trousers and boots.
- **Movement:** Heavy but careful; protective body language; unexpectedly playful.
- **Expression set:** Gentle smile, puzzled look, delighted laugh, protective determination.
- **Distinct from Inigo:** Larger head and shoulders, no thin moustache, softer face, much heavier build.

### Vizzini

- **Silhouette:** Short, compact, animated and theatrical.
- **Face:** High forehead with balding crown, dark hair at sides, pointed eyebrows, smug grin.
- **Clothing:** Elaborate dark green coat with gold embroidery, ruffled collar and cuffs.
- **Movement:** Hands steepled, gesticulating, leaning into conversations.
- **Expression set:** Self-satisfaction, suspicion, mockery, surprise.
- **Avoid:** Making him physically intimidating; his weapon is his supposed intellect.

### Prince Humperdinck

- **Silhouette:** More rigid, formal and imposing than Westley; broad-shouldered aristocrat.
- **Face:** **Older-looking, longer and squarer face**, heavier jaw, sharp nose, narrower eyes, calculating expression.
- **Hair:** **Neatly styled dark ash-brown hair**, combed back with a controlled shape; **not golden blond**.
- **Clothing:** Distinctive **crimson royal cloak**, black-and-gold formal doublet, ornate clasp, embroidered trim and ceremonial belt.
- **Movement:** Upright, controlled, commanding; rarely relaxed.
- **Expression set:** Cold politeness, superiority, irritation, fear beneath composure.
- **Critical distinction:** He must never be mistaken for Westley. Contrast **dark ash-brown groomed hair / square mature face / red-and-gold regal outfit** with **Westley's tousled blond hair / youthful narrow face / black practical outfit**.

### Count Rugen

- **Silhouette:** Tall, lean, severe and disciplined.
- **Face:** Long angular face, narrow eyes, stern expression.
- **Hair:** Dark, slicked-back, slightly greying at temples.
- **Clothing:** High-collared black or midnight-blue military attire with restrained silver details and gloves.
- **Props:** Menacing experimental apparatus only when narratively relevant; preserve his distinctive six-fingered right hand where visible.
- **Expression set:** Clinical curiosity, contempt, controlled alarm.

### Miracle Max and Valerie

- **Miracle Max:** Elderly, wiry, lively face, unruly silver hair, brown cap and rustic layers; exaggerated mischievous expressions.
- **Valerie:** Elderly, silver hair beneath a red headscarf, warm but formidable gaze, practical rustic clothes.
- **Together:** Affectionate bickering and lively comic energy; a cosy, cluttered cottage setting.

### Grandfather and Grandson

- **Grandfather:** Elderly, kind eyes, short silver hair, warm cardigan/jacket, an illustrated storybook in hand.
- **Grandson:** Young boy with tousled brown hair, large curious eyes and simple contemporary clothing.
- **Setting:** Warm bedroom or reading nook, softer indoor palette than the fantasy scenes.
- **Narrative role:** Framing device; a visual change of atmosphere is intentional.

## 5. Composition and continuity rules

1. **One clear dramatic focus per illustration.** Show the player what matters in this passage.
2. **Character identity comes first.** Match face shape, hair, clothing and body proportions to the approved sheet.
3. **Use readable silhouettes.** Especially in duels and action scenes, characters should not merge into the background.
4. **Vary camera language.** Establishing wide shots, low-angle heroic shots, medium dialogue shots, expressive close-ups and occasional over-the-shoulder views.
5. **Keep continuity.** Track time of day, injuries, costume changes, props and relative character positions across connected passages.
6. **Reserve space for the Twine interface.** The image sits above narrative text and choices; do not place essential information at the extreme edges.
7. **Keep the humour.** Even tense scenes can have theatrical gestures or characterful reactions.
8. **Avoid accidentally copying a movie still exactly.** Compose fresh illustrations within the fan project's established design language.

## 6. Standard image-generation prompt

Attach the **approved visual reference sheet** and, if available, the character's individual reference portrait. Then use:

```text
Create one cinematic 16:9 illustration for an unofficial fan-made
interactive fiction adaptation of The Princess Bride.

VISUAL STYLE
Vibrant 1980s-inspired anime/manga; crisp expressive linework;
bold cel shading; rich pink, coral, violet, blue and golden lighting;
painterly fantasy backgrounds; warm theatrical adventure tone.
Match the attached visual style guide, not photorealistic film stills.

SCENE
[Describe the exact narrative moment and location.]

CHARACTERS
[List visible characters. Preserve their approved reference-sheet
faces, hairstyles, silhouettes, clothing and distinctive features.]

COMPOSITION
[Shot type, camera angle, foreground/background and focal point.]

EMOTION / LIGHTING
[Expressions, atmosphere, time of day and lighting direction.]

CONTINUITY
[Relevant costume, props, injuries and details from adjacent scenes.]

OUTPUT
16:9 landscape; no writing, captions, speech bubbles, panel borders,
logos, interface elements or watermarks.
```

### Example — Inigo at the cliffs

```text
Using the attached Princess Bride style guide, create a cinematic
16:9 anime illustration. Inigo Montoya waits at the top of the
Cliffs of Insanity with his ornate rapier drawn. He has shoulder-length
wavy dark-brown hair, a narrow moustache, an angular face, an off-white
shirt and a brown leather vest. Low-angle medium-wide composition,
strong wind, enormous cliffs behind him, vivid coral-and-violet sunset.
His expression is courteous but intensely focused. Clean cel shading,
crisp linework, dramatic warm rim lighting. No text or borders.
```

## 7. Production workflow

1. Keep this file and the approved character reference sheet under version control.
2. For each passage, record a **scene ID**, viewpoint, location, characters, action, emotion, composition and continuity notes.
3. Generate a 16:9 image using this guide and the relevant reference images.
4. Review character consistency before accepting the illustration; regenerate or edit if it drifts.
5. Save assets under `assets/images/` using stable names such as `scene-008-inigo-cliffs.webp`.
6. Keep dialogue and branching choices in Twine/SugarCube, **not baked into the illustration**.
7. Optimise image dimensions and file sizes for web use; preserve original high-resolution masters separately.

## 8. Quality-control checklist

- [ ] Characters match their approved facial features and silhouettes.
- [ ] Westley and Humperdinck are clearly distinguishable.
- [ ] Inigo's expressions do not accidentally resemble Fezzik.
- [ ] Costumes, props and scene geography match neighbouring passages.
- [ ] Vibrant colour and cel-shaded rendering match the established style.
- [ ] No embedded text, logos or interface elements.
- [ ] The illustration works as a landscape image above Twine's narrative.
- [ ] The resulting asset is compressed and named consistently.
